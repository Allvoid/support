Support the projects

A page for voluntary donations to an independent developer: the Arsound app and RimWorld mods.

This is not a shop

Nothing is sold here. Every project is free for everyone, with or without a donation. A donation is a voluntary gift to the developer: it unlocks no features, gives no advantages and creates no obligations beyond those in the public donation offer.

How to donate

Any amount from 100 to 100000 RUB per payment. The money goes to development: time for new versions, test devices and infrastructure. After the payment a self-employed receipt is sent to the e-mail you give.

The payment link will appear once a payment service is connected.

By donating you accept the public offer. A donation is refunded only in the cases of section 5 of the offer.
