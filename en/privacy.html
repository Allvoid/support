2. This site

The site is static: no accounts, forms, cookies, visit counters, ads or third-party analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web server, GitHub may log technical request data (IP address, browser, time) under its own policy — the Recipient has no access to it.

3. Donations

For a donation we process the amount, date and status of the payment, the operation ID, and the e-mail you gave for the receipt. Payment happens on the payment service's side; we never receive or store your card number, CVC or cardholder name. The payment service processes data under its own policy.

To issue a receipt, payment details are entered into the "My Tax" app of the Federal Tax Service of Russia.

4. Purpose and retention

Data is used only to accept the payment, send the receipt, answer requests and meet tax and accounting duties. It is not sold or passed to third parties, except the payment service and the tax service as needed for the payment and reporting. Payment records are kept for the statutory tax and accounting period (at least 4 years); requests — as long as needed to resolve them.

5. Your rights

You may ask what data we hold, and ask to correct or delete it unless the law requires keeping it. Write to the contacts below; we reply within 10 business days.

6. The projects themselves

Arsound and the RimWorld mods do not send data to the Recipient. What the app sends to third-party services (for example track and artist names to Last.fm for recommendations) is described in the project README; these features are user-configurable.

7. Changes

The current version is always on this page. A new version takes effect when published.
