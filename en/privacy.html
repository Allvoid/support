Privacy policy

Version of October 27, 2026. The Russian version prevails in case of discrepancy.

1. Operator

The data operator is The projects' author, self-employed. Contacts are at the bottom of the page.

2. This site

The site is static: no accounts, cookies, visit counters, ads or third-party analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web server, GitHub may log technical request data (IP address, browser, time) under its own policy — the operator has no access to it.

The payment form sends only the chosen amount to the operator's server (arapplications.ru). The server keeps a technical request log (IP address, time, page address) against abuse for no longer than 30 days.

3. The @ArapplicationsBot bot

When you write to the bot, it processes: your Telegram ID, name and username, language; the texts of your requests (bugs, ideas, payment questions) and the conversation about them; your Early access status (whether you have it and since when); the tags you proposed for the discussion chat. Files, logs, videos and screenshots you attach stay on Telegram's servers: the bot forwards them to the developer inside Telegram and does not download them. Bot data is stored on the operator's server; backups are kept for 14 days.

Telegram processes your data under its own policy.

4. Payments

We process the amount, date, status and ID of a payment and its link to your Telegram ID — to open Early access. You enter the e-mail or phone for the receipt on the payment page of the payment service (the payment acceptance operator; its name and terms are shown on that page). We never receive or store your card number, CVC or cardholder name. The payment service and Telegram (for Stars) process data under their own policies. For the receipt, payment details are entered into the "My Tax" app of the Russian Federal Tax Service.

5. Purposes, grounds and retention

Purposes: accepting payments and issuing receipts, rendering the "Early access" service, handling requests, tax duties, protection against abuse. Grounds: the contract under the offer concluded at your initiative, legal duties and your consent (Article 6(1)(1, 2, 5) of Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ). By paying or sending a request to the bot you consent to the processing described here.

Data is not sold or passed to third parties, except the payment service, Telegram and the tax service, as needed for these purposes. Payment records are kept for the tax reporting period (at least 4 years); requests and conversations — as long as needed to resolve them, or less on your request.

6. Your rights

You may ask what data we hold, ask to correct or delete it (unless the law requires keeping it) and withdraw consent. Write to the bot or the e-mail below; we reply within 10 business days. You may also complain to Roskomnadzor, the Russian data protection authority.

7. The projects themselves

Arsound and the RimWorld mods do not send data to the operator. What the app sends to third-party services (for example track and artist names to Last.fm for recommendations) is described in the project README; these features are user-configurable.

8. Changes

The current version is always on this page and takes effect when published.
