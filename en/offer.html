Public offer for a voluntary donation

Last updated: October 26, 2026. The Russian version prevails in case of discrepancy.

The projects are free for everyone. This document governs voluntary donations only: they are not payment for goods, work or services and give no additional rights or features.

1. Terms

1.1. Recipient — Matvey Popov, an individual under the Russian special tax regime "Tax on Professional Income" (self-employed), taxpayer ID 231521307407.

1.2. Donor — a legally capable individual who voluntarily transfers money.

1.3. Projects — the Arsound app (open-source patches for the Android SoundCloud app) and the RimWorld mods listed on the main page.

1.4. Donation — a gratuitous, voluntary transfer of money to the Recipient for the purposes in clause 2.2.

2. Subject

2.1. This is a public offer to conclude a donation agreement under Articles 572 and 582 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation.

2.2. A donation supports and develops the Projects — free, open-source software available to an unlimited number of people at no cost (a public-benefit purpose): development time, testing, test devices, and servers and services needed for building and distribution.

2.3. A donation is voluntary and gratuitous. It is not payment for goods, work or services and does not oblige the Recipient to provide any goods, services, extra or priority features, advantages or other consideration. The Projects are available to everyone equally and free of charge, regardless of whether or how much was donated.

2.4. The agreement is concluded when the donation is transferred; the transfer is full and unconditional acceptance of this offer.

2.5. Published at https://allvoid.github.io/support/en/offer.html.

3. Amount and payment

3.1. The Donor chooses the amount, from 100 to 100000 RUB per payment.

3.2. Payment is made in Russian rubles through payment services available at the time. Card details are entered on the payment service's side; the Recipient does not receive them.

3.3. Before paying, the Donor gives an e-mail address to which a receipt is sent as required by the professional income tax law.

3.4. The donation is made when the payment service confirms the operation.

4. Rights and obligations

4.1. The Recipient will use the funds for the purposes in clause 2.2, send a receipt to the given address and handle the Donor's data under the Privacy Policy.

4.2. The Recipient may spend donations at their discretion within those purposes, without reporting to the Donor.

4.3. The Donor confirms the transfer is voluntary, understands it is gratuitous and expects nothing in return.

4.4. The Donor guarantees the funds are lawfully theirs. A Donor under 18 donates with the consent of a parent or guardian.

5. Refunds

5.1. Because a donation is gratuitous, it is generally not refundable.

5.2. A refund is possible in exceptional cases: a double or erroneous charge caused by a technical failure; a wrong amount caused by an obvious technical error — if requested within a reasonable time.

5.3. To request a refund, write to the e-mail in "Contacts and details" with the date, amount and the e-mail the receipt went to. Do not post these details in public places (chats, trackers). Review takes up to 7 business days. The refund goes back by the same method.

6. Final provisions

6.1. Governed by the law of the Russian Federation.

6.2. Disputes are settled by negotiation; failing that, under the legislation of the Russian Federation.

6.3. The Recipient may change this offer. The current version is always at the address in clause 2.5; changes do not affect donations already made.
